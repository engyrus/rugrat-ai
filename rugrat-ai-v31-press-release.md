# Rugrat.ai v3.1 Achieves New Benchmark in Refusal Accuracy

*Updated Lil' Language Model adds snack-related vocabulary, improved tantrum consistency, and expanded "why" detection*

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**BERLIN, [DATE]** — Rugrat Intelligence, Inc. today announced the release of Rugrat.ai v3.1, a major upgrade to the world's first Lil' Language Model. The new version represents a significant leap forward in the company's mission to deliver AI responses with the full cognitive authority of a 36-month-old human.

"We've been listening to our users," said no one at Rugrat Intelligence. "They told us the model wasn't saying IT'S NOT FAIR enough. We fixed that."

**Key improvements in v3.1 include:**

- **Expanded snack vocabulary** — the model can now express hunger-related grievances with greater specificity
- **Enhanced fairness detection** — prompts containing "fair," "unfair," or any perceived injustice now trigger an appropriate response
- **Improved blame attribution** — the model's "I DIDN'T DO IT" pathway has been retrained for faster, more confident denial
- **Itinerary awareness** — the model now asks ARE WE THERE YET regardless of whether a destination was mentioned
- **Four new Gremlin-class response entities introduced** — NOT ME, IDA KNOW, NOBODY, and O. YEAH now handle blame, confusion, denial, and sarcastic confirmation respectively
- **"Just B. Cause" pathway** — now handles 34% of all why-based queries
- **Rare emotional events** — the "i love you" response, introduced in v3.0, remains at a 5% occurrence rate. Rugrat Intelligence considers this optimal.

Rugrat.ai v3.1 continues to run entirely client-side with no server infrastructure, consistent with the company's belief that backend complexity is, and we quote, DUMB.
