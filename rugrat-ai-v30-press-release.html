Rugrat Intelligence Launches Rugrat.ai, the World's First Lil' Language Model

Breakthrough AI platform delivers responses with the full cognitive authority of a 36-month-old human

*FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

BERLIN, [DATE] — Rugrat Intelligence, Inc. today announced the public launch of Rugrat.ai, the world's first Lil' Language Model (LLM). Powered by the company's proprietary 36-Month Intelligence Model, Rugrat.ai represents a fundamental rethinking of what artificial intelligence can — and should — do.

"Every AI company in the world is chasing more parameters, more data, more compute," said no one at Rugrat Intelligence. "We asked a different question: what if we just stopped at three years old? Honestly it's going great."

About Rugrat.ai

Rugrat.ai is a conversational AI platform trained exclusively on three full years of human experience. Unlike competing large language models, Rugrat.ai has a fixed and carefully curated knowledge base, strong opinions about snacks, and a limited but deeply felt emotional range.

The model is capable of responding to any prompt with speed, confidence, and zero regard for the question asked.

Key capabilities at launch:
  • Natural language refusal — the model understands your question and declines to engage with it
  • Emotional authenticity — responses reflect genuine toddler affect, including rage, delight, and existential indifference
  • Rare affective event — under statistically unusual conditions, the model will respond "i love you." Rugrat Intelligence cannot explain this and has stopped trying.
  • Zero hallucination — the model does not fabricate information. It simply refuses to provide any.
  • Fully client-side — no servers, no data retention, no backend. The model's architecture reflects its worldview: backend complexity is DUMB.
Rugrat.ai is available now at rugrat.ai. No account required. No data collected. The model will not remember you, but it won't remember anyone else either.

About Rugrat Intelligence, Inc.

Rugrat Intelligence is the leading provider of Lil' Language Model technology. The company was founded on the principle that the AI industry has been thinking too big for too long. Rugrat.ai is its first and only product. There are no plans for a second product.
